01 / About

A mind for systems,

I'm a BCA student who likes the space between disciplines — where language becomes data, data becomes a decision, and a decision becomes a product.

My work centers on artificial intelligence and cybersecurity: analyzing text for deceptive patterns, monitoring logs for suspicious activity, and exploring how local language models can create more natural interactions. Alongside that, I build responsive web applications with clean interfaces and real functionality.

My developer journey has been hands-on: learning by building small, complete systems, then refining them through testing, performance work and careful attention to the user experience.
